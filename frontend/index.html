        Sorting errors send a bag down the wrong branch. They apply to every sorter.

        Belt lights

        • Running No attention needed.
        • Stopped Halted by the operator.
        • Warning The belt is congested, or a bag on it has not moved for 30 s.
        • Fault The belt is broken and needs a repair.
        • Blinking An alarm on the belt has not been acknowledged yet.

        Belts and bags

        • Solid blue edges The belt is stopped by the operator.
        • Long amber dashes The belt is congested.
        • Short red dashes The belt is faulty.
        • Clock on a bag The bag has not moved for 30 s.

        Sorters and chutes

        A sorter's gate swings across the line to send the next bag into its branch, as the simulation decides; the light before it turns white while a bag passes. Each chute's counter shows the bags delivered there correctly.

        Check-in queues

        Each passenger in a desk's lane is one bag waiting to go onto its belt. The first one leaves as soon as their bag is taken; +N counts those who do not fit in the lane.

        Destinations

        Each bag's tag shows the code of its exit.
